FAQ

Questions, answered

Can't find what you need? The help centre covers getting started, transfers and staying safe.

How do I get a Fiam account number?
Download Fiam on Android and sign up. Your personal account number is created for you, and you can fund it from any Nigerian bank right away.
Which banks can I send money to?
Any bank account in Nigeria. Pick the bank, enter the account number, review the details and confirm with your PIN.
How is my money protected?
Every transfer needs your 4-digit PIN and a review step. Identity checks set your limits, and you get an instant alert for every credit and debit.
What are account tiers and limits?
Tiers set how much you can move each day. You start on Tier 1 and unlock higher limits by verifying more of your identity in the app.
When is Fiam coming to iPhone?
