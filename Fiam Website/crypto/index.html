DEPOSIT

Receive crypto from any wallet or exchange.

Every Fiam account gets its own deposit address for each coin and network. Share it, or show the QR code, and your balance updates once the network confirms.

  1. 1
    Pick the coin and networkChoose USDT, BTC or ETH, then the network you're sending on.
  2. 2
    Copy your address or scan the QRPaste it into your exchange or wallet's withdraw screen.
  3. 3
    Get credited after confirmationWe notify you when it arrives, with its naira value at that moment.
PAYOUT

Send crypto out, with no surprises.

Pay out to any external wallet. Before you confirm, you see the network fee, how long it should take, and exactly what the recipient will get.

Type or tap an amountUse presets like 25%, 50% or Max.
Paste the addressWe check the format matches the network.
Review the breakdownNetwork fee, arrival time, and what they receive.
Confirm with your PINYou get a receipt with the transaction hash.
