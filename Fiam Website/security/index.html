Staying safe

Fiam will never ask for your PIN.

Not by phone, email, SMS or social media. If someone does, it isn't us.

  1. 01Keep your PIN and one-time codes to yourself.
  2. 02Check the recipient's name on the review screen before you confirm.
  3. 03Be wary of offers that sound too good, or anyone rushing you to pay.
  4. 04Only download Fiam from Google Play or links on this website.
